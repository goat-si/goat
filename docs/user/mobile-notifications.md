# Mobile notifications

GOAT Bot can show local alerts from directly paired computers, including over
Tailscale, while GOAT is running. In **Settings → Notifications**, turn on
**Bot alerts on this device** and allow notifications when your phone asks.
If you previously denied permission, open system notification settings from
the same screen.

On the bot's computer, **Settings → GOAT Bot → Bot alerts** controls which
events alert you. The default, **Needs you**, covers questions, approvals, and
sign-in requests. **Needs you and results** also includes finished and failed
work. **Off** stops those alerts from that computer.

An alert names the bot and what happened. Tap it to open the task and its
request. The iOS app stops receiving alerts when iOS suspends it. Alerts to a
closed phone, Android ongoing agent activity, and iOS Live Activities require
GOAT Connect.
