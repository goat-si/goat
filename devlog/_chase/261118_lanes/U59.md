## Coordinator ruling, #668 Stop fence (verbatim)

Coordinator ruling, U59 #668 Stop fence: recovered resends wait for the command gate.
Policy: no recovered unattended resend may execute until (a) the startup command gate has opened (signalCommandReady, serverRuntimeStartup.ts:857) and (b) the person commands queued during startup have been admitted and committed. Then the resend decision re-reads the run, and if a Stop (or any newer command) committed for it, the resend is dropped, not executed.
Shape (smallest): do not enqueue recovered resend effects during early recovery; record the intent, then a step that runs after signalCommandReady and after the queued-command drain re-checks each run and enqueues only those still eligible, through the normal outbox with the original command id. If enqueueing earlier is unavoidable, the worker must not claim effects of that recovery type before the same gate (an explicit admission fence keyed to command-ready plus drain). Either way, all other startup effects and agents stay exactly as they are (do not delay them).
Bound: the drain is finite (only commands queued before the gate); state this, and do not wait on new commands arriving after it.
Tests (Deferred-gated startup): a Stop queued during startup prevents the resend; with no Stop the resend executes once with the original command id; a duplicate recovery pass does not double-enqueue; other startup effects still run before the gate as they do today. Record this ruling verbatim in U59.md.
