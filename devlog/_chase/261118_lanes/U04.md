## P1 client behavior

- Web/desktop show the saved model after a seeded thread starts and respect an explicit pick. Same-model traits survive aliases; another model's traits do not follow the pick.
- Web/desktop model pickers keep touch keyboards down until the search is tapped. Fine pointers can search immediately. A duplicate model appears once per instance and consumes one jump shortcut.
- Web/desktop suggestions remain clickable from first to last in a short window. Stash can still open and close at the composer.
- Web/desktop/mobile `@` finds older titles on reachable linked hosts and `/model` opens the existing model picker when selected. Blank searches and inactive menus are distinct.
- Web/desktop show a temporarily absent explicit Pi/ACP model as unavailable and retain its traits. Mobile keeps the saved model and traits in its existing picker and send flow.
