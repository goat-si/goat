## Coordinator ruling, cross-environment retarget (verbatim)

Coordinator ruling, U62 #907 cross-environment retarget: preventing the stale overwrite is required (your 4fdf4ee600, accepted). Full simultaneous completion for the same attachment in two environments is NOT required in this lane: the single visible upload state per attachment is pre-existing, so record it in U62.md as a P3 follow-up.
Minimum for environment A's already-retained send, which must hold before READY:
1) It is never lost silently: it ends in the existing visible failure or retry state on that message, never sent without its attachment and never sent with B's upload ID.
2) A retry of that A message uploads to A again (or reuses a still-valid A upload) and can succeed while B's draft keeps its own upload. Add that case to your regression: retarget to B, A completes late, A's message fails visibly, retrying A succeeds, B is unaffected.
3) The stale A upload ID you clean up is released through the normal path, with no orphaned A upload left behind.
If 2 cannot be met without per-environment visible state, report it to me with path:line before READY. Record this ruling verbatim in U62.md.

Test the sequence with your queued regression, adjusted to this rule: retarget to B, A completes late and is ignored, A fails visibly, retry A succeeds and takes ownership, B shows a retryable failed state, retry B succeeds, and no send ever carries the other environment's ID.
