# Coordinator rulings (verbatim)

> Coordinator C4 rulings for U34.
> #665 (16a70050f47): APPROVED as stated. The bridge restartAudioService is optional and Linux desktop only. It restarts once, only after NotFoundError/NotReadableError, and only its own Electron audio utility; it retries after a confirmed exit and honors cancellation. Denied permission, a missing utility, a failed or timed-out restart, or an aborted capture each surface the original error with no retry. An absent bridge keeps today's behavior. Tests cover the once-only retry, cancellation, and absent-bridge cases.
> #898 (4247b5aa1a1): DEFERRED this cycle. GOAT's SpeechInference is batch-only, so porting live streaming means a new wire protocol, capability negotiation and stream lifetime design. That is new feature work, not a catch-up adaptation. Record in U34.md: "#898 deferred — GOAT has batch-only SpeechInference; streaming needs new protocol, capability negotiation and lifetime design; revisit as a dedicated lane." Don't add the per-device streaming preference either; a setting with no behavior would mislead.
> Record these rulings verbatim in U34.md and continue.
