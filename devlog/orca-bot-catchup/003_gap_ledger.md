## Deliberately not adopted

- **Group rooms** (several bots in one room with @mentions, `274_orca/src/main/bots/bot-group-*.ts`): large runtime and UI surface; GOAT's cross-bot Needs you and per-bot Home cover the triage journey. Revisit only on owner request.
- **Composio and Google connectors** (ORCA-B11): third-party credential brokers and Google OAuth apps are external accounts. Per-bot scoping of tools the machine already has (090) comes first.
- **Slack, Gmail and Calendar event triggers** (Orca lists Slack events, `274_orca/src/shared/automation-event-triggers.ts:17`): 040 covers Slack chat routing only; event triggers from Slack and Google wait until 010's model has shipped and been used.
- **Orca's cloud connector service** (`274_orca/cloud/`): GOAT stays without a hosted service until GOAT Connect; triggers use GOAT's own webhook endpoint.
- **Agents asking member bots**: excluded by D4's recommendation; revisit as report-only under the agent's own scope.
